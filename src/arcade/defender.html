Rescue falling humanoids by catching them with your ship and setting them down on the ground. Smart bombs clear the screen; hyperspace is an emergency escape with a chance of destroying your ship. The cabinet buttons also work with a mouse or touch; drag the joystick vertically to fly.

Original game: Williams Electronics. Game and sound ROMs assembled from mwenge/defender (Eugene Jarvis and Sam Dicker), verified against all twelve reference checksums.
