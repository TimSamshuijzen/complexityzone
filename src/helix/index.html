Mouse — drag empty space to orbit · wheel to zoom · right-drag to pan
Turn — drag the helix, the dial or the knurled knob · click the black lever to lock or release
Sliders — drag a red or blue ball along its rod · click a bare rod to put a slider on it
Keys — Space lock/release · R record next (⇧R all) · B blue circle (⇧B auto-balance) · T test balance · S read next (⇧S all) · J join · ← → rods (⇧ ±1°) · 1 2 3 modes · O V E D F views · Esc stop
How it works — rod k points at angle φk = nωtk and carries the sample f(tk) as a distance. The centre of mass of all sliders is therefore ½(an, bn): gravity computes the Fourier sum and turns that point to the bottom.
